Delete your account

Sayloo · Last updated: October 26, 2026 · עברית

In the app

  • Open the parents' area.
  • On the "Account" card, tap "Delete account" and confirm.
  • An internet connection is needed.

Without the app

Write to us at sayloo.app@gmail.com from the e-mail address of the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete the account. We will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • The account (name and e-mail address), the children's profiles, settings, progress and usage data.
  • On the phone where you delete it: that account's profiles, progress and recordings.
  • The children's voice recordings never leave the phone. They are deleted when you delete the account in the app, the child, or the app itself.

The information is deleted within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep, and backup copies, which are deleted in their normal cycle. More in the Privacy Policy.
