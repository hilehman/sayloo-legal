Privacy Policy

Sayloo · Last updated: November 1, 2026

In short

  • Your child's voice is never recorded and never leaves the phone. Each word is checked on the device as it is said, then forgotten.
  • We do not keep your child's real name, date of birth, photo or location.
  • There are no ads, and we do not sell information to anyone.

Who we are

Sayloo is a language-learning app for children from age 3. The app is operated by Sayloo ("we"). For any question or request about privacy: sayloo.app@gmail.com.

What stays only on the phone

Your child's voice is not kept at all: the app listens when it is your child's turn to speak, checks on the phone which sounds it heard, and discards the voice at once. What stays on the phone is only how each word went (whether it was said, and how each of its sounds came out), to show you the progress. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the child or the account in the app, or delete the app itself.

What we keep

  • The parent's account: the name and e-mail address of the Google account you signed in with, and whether the account has the full version of the app (which we set).
  • The child's profile: a nickname and an avatar you chose, and whether to address the child as a boy or a girl (only so the texts shown to you are worded correctly). We recommend a nickname rather than a full name.
  • Progress: levels and words learned, so that progress is kept on a new phone too.
  • Usage data: when the app was used (to the hour), how long children played, which games and levels, how many attempts and successes there were, and technical details such as the device model, the app version, and the IP address in the server's logs. This data is linked to the child's profile in your account.
  • Error reports: what went wrong and where, without personal information.

Why we use the information

  • To run the app and keep progress.
  • To understand how children play and improve the games: length, difficulty, and what happens when children stop playing.
  • To improve speech recognition and fix errors.
  • To decide what each account may open, enforce the terms of use, protect our rights and comply with the law.

We do not use the information for advertising, and we do not build a marketing profile from it.

Who the information is shared with

We do not sell information or pass it on for advertising. We use service providers that store and process the information for us only:

  • Supabase: server storage, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Google: signing in to the account.

If paid features are added in the future, the payment provider will receive what is needed to process the payment. We will give information to authorities if the law requires us to, or when needed to protect our rights or someone's safety. If the app is transferred to another party, for example to a company that will run it, the information will move with it and stay subject to this policy.

How long the information is kept

  • Account, profiles and progress: as long as the account exists.
  • Detailed usage data: up to 12 months. After that, only general summaries remain, not linked to any child.
  • After an account is deleted: the information is deleted within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep or what is needed to settle a dispute, and backup copies, which are deleted in their normal cycle.

Your rights

  • To see and get a copy of the information kept about you and your children.
  • To correct wrong information.
  • To delete: deleting a child in the parents' area deletes its data on our side too. The whole account, with the children's profiles and progress, can be deleted in the parents' area (Account, then Delete account) or by writing to us.

You can send us any such request at sayloo.app@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is meant for children who play under a parent's supervision. The parent is the one who signs in with a Google account, creates the child's profile and agrees to this policy. Children do not open an account or give any details themselves.

Your consent

You are under no legal duty to give us information, but without the information described here we cannot run the app. By signing in to and using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here, including its storage with providers in the European Union.

Security

The information is encrypted in transit, and access to it is restricted: each account sees only its own information. No method is perfect and we cannot guarantee complete security. If a security incident affects your information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change the policy, we will update this page and the date at its top. We will announce a material change in the app.
